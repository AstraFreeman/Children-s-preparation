\caption{Three-tier architecture of the MRQS instrument. Tier~A items assess empirical design features derived from CONSORT \cite{Schulz2010CONSORT}, Cochrane \cite{HigginsThomas2019}, and GRADE \cite{GRADE} standards and are required for meta-analytic synthesis. Tier~B items assess reporting transparency following CONSORT~2010 \cite{Schulz2010CONSORT} guidelines. Tier~C items capture domain-specific concerns based on \citeauthor{Deterding2011}~\cite{Deterding2011} and \citeauthor{Buckley2018}~\cite{Buckley2018}. The design dependency gradient indicates that Tier~A items are inapplicable to qualitative and design-based studies, limiting the maximum achievable score.}
